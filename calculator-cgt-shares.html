How to use the Capital Gains Tax calculator for Shares

To use the calculator, enter the following details about the shares you sold:

  • Ownership > 12 months — enables the 50% CGT discount if applicable.

  • Purchase price — The total amount you paid for each share investment parcel.

  • Sold price — The total amount you received for each share investment parcel.

  • Costs of Purchase/Selling — Transaction costs, brokerage fees, etc.

  • Carry-forward capital losses — Capital losses from prior years that can be applied before any CGT discount.

  • Current Taxable Income — Your taxable income before the capital gain.
